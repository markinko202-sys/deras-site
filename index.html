KILAT

kilat — /ki·lat/ — lightning.

Swift, like water
after rain.

Deras is what Malaysians call a river after the monsoon — fast, full, impossible to hold back. We named a car after it.

We build for a country that drives through hujan lebat at a hundred and ten, climbs to Genting in the fog, and crosses the Penang bridge at midnight with the windows down. Not a copy of Stuttgart. Not a copy of Tokyo. Kita punya.
